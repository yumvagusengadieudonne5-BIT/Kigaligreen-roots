# Product overview

## Purpose

Invite Kigali residents to join a recurring community tree-planting initiative. The landing page should make the impact, activities, and next planting-day details easy to understand, then make sign-up straightforward.

## Audience

- Kigali residents, students, families, and community groups.
- People who may be new to tree planting and need reassurance that no experience is required.
- Prospective volunteers who want clear event details and a quick way to register.

## Core message

“Your city, your environment, your future.” Residents plant together on the second Saturday of each month to green neighborhoods and support a healthier Kigali.

## Content observed on the reference site

- Hero: “Together, Let’s Grow a Greener, Healthier Kigali.”
- Benefits: cleaner air and shade; community spirit; practical skills.
- Planting-day activities: tree planting, urban gardening, and tree-care basics.
- Event information: second Saturday of the month, 8:00–11:00 AM, Kigali Neighborhood Green Zone.
- Sign-up fields: full name, phone/WhatsApp number, and neighborhood.
- Contact options: phone and WhatsApp, plus Kigali, Rwanda.

Treat schedule, location, and contact details as content that should be verified with the site owner before changing. Do not invent dates, outcomes, impact statistics, or registration behavior.

## Primary action

“Join the Next Planting Day” / “Count Me In” should direct visitors to the sign-up form. The page should also offer a clear WhatsApp contact option for people who prefer to ask questions.
