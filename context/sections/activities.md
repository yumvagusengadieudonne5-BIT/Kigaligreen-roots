# Activities

**Planned component:** `src/components/sections/activities-section.tsx`

**Anchor:** `activities`

## Content

Explain that no experience is needed and volunteer leads guide each group. Include:

1. **Tree planting** — plant and mulch saplings along streets, at schools, and in shared spaces.
2. **Urban gardening** — establish small vegetable or flower beds in the neighborhood.
3. **Tree-care basics** — learn watering, staking, and other simple care.

## Behavior and layout

- Use clear, scannable activity cards.
- Do not imply that participants need prior knowledge or special equipment.
- Keep icons decorative when the card heading and copy already communicate the activity.

## Accessibility

- Preserve a logical heading hierarchy.
- Avoid communicating the activity sequence using numbers or icons alone.
