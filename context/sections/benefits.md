# Benefits

## Content

Introduce why people should join, followed by three benefit cards:

1. **Cleaner Air & Shade** — help cool streets and improve neighborhood air quality.
2. **Community Spirit** — meet neighbors, students, and local volunteers.
3. **Practical Skills** — learn urban gardening and tree-care techniques.

## Behavior and layout

- Keep explanations concise and community-centered.
- Use a responsive card layout that becomes a single column on narrow screens.

## Accessibility

- Use a section heading and meaningful card headings.
- Ensure card meaning is conveyed in text, not by color or icon alone.
