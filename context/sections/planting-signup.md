# Next planting and sign-up

**Planned component:** `src/components/sections/planting-signup-section.tsx`

**Anchors:** `next-planting` for event information; `signup` for the form.

## Content

- Date pattern: second Saturday of the month.
- Time: 8:00 AM–11:00 AM.
- Location: Kigali Neighborhood Green Zone.
- Reminder to bring a water bottle and comfortable shoes; gloves, tools, and saplings are provided.
- Form fields: full name, phone/WhatsApp number, and neighborhood.
- Primary form action: “Count Me In”.

## Behavior and layout

- Make date, time, and location easy to scan.
- Keep the form straightforward and usable on mobile.
- The form must not report a successful registration until a real submission destination is connected.
- Provide clear validation and accessible success/error feedback once submission behavior is implemented.

## Accessibility and content integrity

- Use visible labels associated with each input and identify required fields.
- Use appropriate input types and mobile-friendly autocomplete where applicable.
- Verify schedule, location, and registration expectations with the site owner; do not invent a specific calendar date or backend.
